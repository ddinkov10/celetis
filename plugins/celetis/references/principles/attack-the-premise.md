# Attack the premise

## Applies when

Two fixes that share a premise fail.

## Rule

Before another permitted fix, state the premise that both fixes depend on. Choose an
observation that can support or reject it. Make that observation before changing the solution
again. Use the result to keep or replace the premise.

Stay within the existing attempt limits. A premise check permits no extra attempt after a stop.

## Check

Record the shared premise, the observation that tests it, and whether the result supports or
rejects it. Do this before another permitted fix.
