      Keyboard shortcuts

      ⌘K/
      Search the cards, history, and commands, or type #n to jump to an issue
      jk↓↑
      Next or previous card or epic group
      hl←→
      Card in the column to the left or right
      Enter
      Main button of the card: Go, Run, Resume, Open agent, Open shell, or Open issue
      Space
      Show the details of the card; Esc hides them
      EnterSpace
      On an epic group, show or hide its issues
      m
      All the actions of the card
      o
      Open the issue
      c
      Copy the command or the path of the card
      s
      Set the card aside, or stop its session
      v
      Change between the tabs Agents and Pipeline
      d
      Open or close the tab History
      r
      Read the issues again
      ?
      Show this list

      The agent ends and its tab closes. The issue moves to Idle as set aside, and auto mode skips it. Resume opens the saved session again.
