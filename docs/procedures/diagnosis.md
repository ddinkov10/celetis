# The diagnosis

The explore and the diagnosis of `bug` and `hotfix`. Each lane applies "The explore" at step 3,
after it reads the project context, and "The diagnosis" at step 4.

## The explore

Explore the files, the documents, and the recent commits that the problem touches. The traps of the
project context are the constraints of the fix.

## The diagnosis

During the diagnosis, after a substantive correction, after two failed fixes of the same problem,
and before an existing repair limit stops work, apply `<plugin>/docs/procedures/lesson-capture.md`.
Reuse a checkpoint already handled in the turn.

Read each principle below. Apply each one whose `Applies when` holds during the diagnosis, with its
`Check`:

- `<plugin>/docs/principles/attack-the-premise.md`: read it before another already-permitted
  attempt. Every existing attempt limit and stop still applies.
- `<plugin>/docs/principles/explain-the-number.md`.

A hypothesis that rests on library, platform, or vendor behavior is checked per
`<plugin>/docs/procedures/docs-lookup.md` before the report names it the root cause.
